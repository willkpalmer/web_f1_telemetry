    Channels

    Compare cars through a single lap, aligned by distance travelled so you can read them corner for corner. The time-delta panel shows how far each car is ahead of or behind the reference driver at every point of the lap. OpenF1 has no steering-angle channel, so that one can't be shown.

    Each driver's gap to the race leader, lap by lap. The leader is whoever is actually ahead on track, even if they aren't ticked. Most useful for Race and Sprint sessions.
